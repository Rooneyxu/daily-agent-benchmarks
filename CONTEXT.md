# Bio & Medical Benchmark Catalog

## Language

**Public Catalog**: The public collection of biology or medicine benchmark papers whose title and abstract clearly identify a qualifying contribution.

_Avoid_: Main area, formal zone.

**Review Queue**: Internal records whose title and abstract mention a relevant benchmark but do not make the paper's contribution explicit. These records are not published on the website.

_Avoid_: Public watchlist.

**Topic**: The benchmark's primary subject area. Each public record has one topic: Bio/Medical & Text, Multimodal, Experiment & Agents, or Biosafety.

_Avoid_: Category when referring to a contribution type.

**Contribution Type**: What the paper contributes to benchmarking. Each public record has one type: New Benchmark, Benchmark Methodology, or Benchmark Audit.

_Avoid_: Priority, P0, P1, P2.

**New Benchmark**: A paper whose title or abstract explicitly releases or introduces a benchmark, dataset, evaluation suite, challenge set, or test set.

**Benchmark Methodology**: A paper whose title or abstract explicitly studies benchmark construction, automated question generation, verification, quality control, grading, or evaluation methodology.

**Benchmark Audit**: A paper whose title or abstract explicitly audits, reproduces, tests contamination or leakage in, or otherwise evaluates the reliability of a benchmark.

**Admission Evidence**: Signals found in the title and abstract that determine whether a record enters the Public Catalog.

**Enrichment Evidence**: Signals found in full text that add traceable evidence or refine the topic. Enrichment Evidence never promotes a record into the Public Catalog.
